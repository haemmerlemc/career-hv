Transformation

Chief of Staff / Transformation Lead (f/m/d)

Lustenau, Vorarlberg Min. 4 days on-site Travel involved

I'm Lara, and I'm looking for a Chief of Staff.

We're Hämmerle & Vogel — a 60-year-old, family-owned embroidery company. We design and produce embroidery for premium lingerie, couture, and ready-to-wear for some of the most loved brands in the world like Victoria's Secret, Agent Provocateur, Aubade, Marks & Spencer and many more. I've taken over as the third generation, and I'm rebuilding it customer-first, enabled by technology.

What's the job?

Half the job is making my impact on the business more effective: running cadence, prepping decisions, catching what's slipping, freeing me up to do the work I should be doing. The other half is actively transforming the business with me, building the things that change how we work, sitting with our team and our customers, finding the friction, fixing it.

It might not be the classic Chief of Staff role as it's very builder heavy. You're a mini-CEO who's understanding the company front to back and building things as we learn.

What you'll spend your time on
  • You hold the threads and keep track of what's moving, what's stuck, what needs a decision.
  • You own a portfolio of strategic projects (e.g., internal collaboration infrastructure, sales-design process redesign, hiring sprints) and drive them to outcomes.
  • You're building the tools and automations that change how the company actually runs and how we best serve our customers.
Who I think you are
  • You want to learn what it takes to turnaround a traditional manufacturing business.
  • You're structured and you believe excellence comes from figuring out the details.
  • You are customer-obsessed and always asking: do our customers have what they need? How can we improve their experience?
  • You're at the intersection of "I want to try new AI tools" and "I want to apply them to real problems in real companies."
  • You can hold the bridge in your head between a traditional business and new technology and you respect that they're two different worlds that need translation.
  • You're an excellent communicator, you write clearly in English. German is a strong plus. French is the ultimate.
  • You've worked some time in a startup-ish environment.
  • A plus: You do love beautiful things, after all we're creating amazing designs here.

I genuinely think this is one of the best places in the world right now to learn three things at once: what AI can actually do when you apply it to the real world, how you bring people along through real change, and how you transform a legacy business.

What I offer
  • An amazing learning opportunity: the chance to transform a business.
  • Annual learning budget. Access to a modern tech stack of AI tools.
  • Located in Vorarlberg, Austria. 15 minutes from Lake Constance, 1.5 hours from Zurich, 2 from Munich, 3.5 from Milan. Relocation support if needed.
  • Minimum 4 days a week at the office + travel.
  • Competitive salary with a success-based upside.
