Junior Designer (f/m/d)

Full-time Lustenau, Vorarlberg Hybrid

Hämmerle & Vogel is a 60-year-old, family-owned embroidery manufacturer in Vorarlberg, Austria. We design and produce embroideries for some of the most loved fashion and lingerie brands in the world: Victoria's Secret, Marks & Spencer, Agent Provocateur, Akris, and many more. What our team designs here, people wear around the world.

We're in the middle of rethinking what embroidery design can be. So we are opening our business up to new ideas, new technology, and new ways of working. We're looking for a junior designer to be part of that: someone creative and open-minded, who wants to push the creative boundaries of embroidery design.

You don't need to know embroidery — we'll teach you the craft and the technique. What you do bring is real talent: you draw or paint exceptionally well, you're curious and a fast thinker, and you're comfortable working with the different people involved in the design process.

What you'll do
  • You design: bringing fresh ideas and your own creative point of view to embroideries for international brands.
  • You turn ideas into sketches with a strong story and point of view.
  • You learn the craft hands-on, from a team with decades of embroidery expertise, and grow into the technique over time.
  • You work on real collections for real brands, from first concept toward the finished design.
  • You experiment, question, and help us discover what embroidery design can become.
What you bring
  • You draw and paint exceptionally well — sketching by hand is second nature, and you're fast.
  • A strong creative eye and your own design instinct — you treat design as creative problem-solving, and you've got the confidence to challenge ideas and bring your own. We'd love to see it in a portfolio (any medium: textile, fashion, graphic, illustration, art).
  • A fast, curious mind: you want to push the boundaries of embroidery design, you like trying new things, and you're not attached to "how it's always been done."
  • You keep your work organised and know what to prioritise, even when several projects move at once.
  • You get things done: you carry an idea through to a finished design, you deliver even when it's busy, and you find your own way around obstacles.
  • Openness to new technology, including AI, as part of how we design — and a drive to keep learning.
  • You work closely with an experienced design team and learn from them daily, you can hold your own with senior people and partners from other fields, and you're genuinely curious about what the customer needs.
What we offer
  • The chance to learn embroidery design from the ground up, from a team that's among the best at the craft.
  • Work with international fashion and lingerie brands.
  • A company actively rethinking how it designs — real room to experiment, and to work with new tools and AI as we do.
  • Mentorship and a genuine learning path, not busywork.
  • Annual learning budget.
  • A salary starting at €45,000 gross per year.
  • A vertical company, from design to finished product — embroidery up close.

Scope: Full-time.
